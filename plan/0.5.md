# 0.5 — Concurrency safety foundations

Prioritize a coherent extension of safety before expanding the concurrency runtime.
The proposed architecture and unresolved decisions are in
[the concurrency design note](concurrency.md).

The current native task boundary supports checked loans of plain referents,
including receipt-preserving virtual submission and completion. It does not yet
transfer owning payloads or establish general race freedom. Direct reference
access bounds can now be declared and checked on contracts. Continue with:

1. Formalize access regions and extend authority analysis to owner payloads,
   latent cleanup, unknown aliases and cross-thread resource requirements.
2. Preserve checked access and execution requirements through virtual contracts,
   including affinity, foreign effects and captured provider identities.
3. Transport pending execution obligations through opaque storage and owning
   aggregates, including failure paths and exactly-once cleanup.
4. Add certified storage partitioning rather than relying on general dynamic-index
   proofs. Preserve nonoverlap through views, calls and moves.
5. Define structured task ownership, completion publication and result
   observation separately; cover failed joins and cancellation without abandoning
   execution obligations.
6. Build verified synchronization and protected storage for guards and channels,
   with the necessary atomic operations and memory-order guarantees.
7. Specify backend-independent waiting and cooperative cancellation, including
   registration lifetime, notification races and resource reclamation.
8. Add reusable workers and validate with an application once those obligations
   are checked. Distinguish parallel, serialized and thread-confined execution.
   Evaluate green threads without committing to a suspension representation now.

Integrate System.executor after native-only virtual methods can coexist with
System-based VM and compile-time execution. This is a separate implementation
constraint, not a reason to bypass the safety foundations.

This is the development order, not a commitment to finish every stage in 0.5.

Unscheduled: explicit closure captures, module runtime initialization,
slot-owner/suballocation APIs and additional platforms. Other topics have their
own notes in this directory.
