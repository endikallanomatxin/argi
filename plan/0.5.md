# 0.5 — Capability views and concurrent safety

Argi permits mutable aliasing within an execution. The checker preserves
temporal validity, storage generations and cleanup obligations. Concurrent
sharing and ownership transfer add distinct requirements to those existing
checks. Use explicit trust at the type owner's boundary to certify concurrent
operations.

## Implementation order

1. **Bind capabilities in Virtual.** Resolve contract evidence at construction.
   Erase the complete captured reference type, including its access mode.
   Derive each member's residual signature and translate its effects and
   output dependencies. Bind multiple arguments against one borrowed context
   and preserve each capture's lifetime and overlap constraints. Keep the
   context-and-table representation and evaluate the descriptor once.
2. **Share storage explicitly.** Introduce `$$&` backed by `ConcurrentAlias`.
   Transition structural storage to shared mode and invalidate ordinary access
   through owners, aliases and containers. Follow structural subobjects and
   keep external referents under the contract author's guarantee. Restore
   ordinary access with a fresh generation after certified completion.
3. **Propagate value evidence.** Reuse value facts, mutation tracking and
   reusable summaries. Carry evidence through erasure, forwarding and
   aggregates. At joins and unknown writes retain only guarantees established
   for every alternative. Keep evidence out of type identity, runtime layouts
   and specialization keys.
4. **Transfer ownership.** Require a value proof, valid retained dependencies,
   compatible authority and a cleanup path at submission. Use the accepted
   native handoff to retire sender cleanup exactly once. Preserve cleanup on
   failed start. Verify return and discard paths using certified completion.
5. **Integrate and simplify.** Exercise local and concurrent deallocation with
   one nominal String type. Verify mutex access, stale aliases, pending loans,
   capture overlap, cleanup and owner transfer, including rejection of a String
   with a local deallocator at the transfer boundary. Remove checks and
   inference made redundant by the explicit sharing and transfer rules.

[Virtual capability binding](virtual_capabilities.md) and
[static transfer evidence](transferability.md) specify the integration cases.
The [resource authority research](resource_authority.md) records prior
experiments. General protocol inference and ordered resource transformers are
outside the implementation gates above. Pending sequential safety
counterexamples remain independent requirements.
