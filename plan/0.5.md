# 0.5 — Concurrency safety foundations

Prioritize a coherent extension of safety before expanding the concurrency runtime.
The proposed architecture and unresolved decisions are in
[the concurrency design note](concurrency.md).

1. Specify access regions, authority and transitions independently of executors.
   Implement access analysis with positive sequential aliasing cases and negative
   concurrent-conflict cases.
2. Check transitive execution effects through globals, cleanup, external resources
   and virtual contracts, preserving the distinction between validity and access.
3. Define checked transfer of ownership and authority through ordinary and opaque
   storage, including contextual resource requirements and cross-thread lifetimes.
4. Formalize single execution, completion publication, result extraction and
   cleanup. Establish the ownership model for structured borrowed tasks and
   distinguish execution ownership from result observation.
5. Build verified synchronization and protected storage for guards and channels,
   with the necessary atomic operations and memory-order guarantees.
6. Specify backend-independent waiting and cooperative cancellation, including
   registration lifetime, notification races and resource reclamation.
7. Add reusable workers and validate with an application once those obligations
   are checked. Evaluate green threads later without committing to a suspension
   representation now.

Integrate System.executor after native-only virtual methods can coexist with
System-based VM and compile-time execution. This is a separate implementation
constraint, not a reason to bypass the safety foundations.

This is the development order, not a commitment to finish every stage in 0.5.

Unscheduled: explicit closure captures, module runtime initialization,
slot-owner/suballocation APIs and additional platforms. Other topics have their
own notes in this directory.
