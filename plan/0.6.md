# 0.6 — Workload-driven exploration

Choose concrete workloads before extending representations or public APIs.

- Port a compiler component to Argi; assess self-hosting and reproducible bootstrap.
- Explore GPU execution and numerical/scientific libraries.
- Measure additional VM/JIT optimization tiers.
- Evaluate packed choices/arrays, SoA, ropes, and multidimensional views.
  Overlapping choice payloads require coordinated layout, constants, addresses,
  memory encoding and both backends; cleanup identities stay separate from
  physical addresses.
- Evaluate a foreign virtual ABI, inlining/unrolling controls, and advanced protocols.

These are candidates, not release commitments. Other unscheduled ideas remain
in `core/library_ideas.txt` and the language design sketches.
